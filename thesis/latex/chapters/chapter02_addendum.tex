\section{Neural Time-Series Forecasting and Production MLOps}

Recent traffic forecasting literature emphasises decomposition linear models~\cite{zeng2023transformers}, patch-based Transformers~\cite{nie2023patchtst}, and graph spatio-temporal networks~\cite{yu2018stgcn}. For deployment, smart-city platforms increasingly combine stream ingestion, model registries, and latency-aware inference rather than offline notebooks alone~\cite{its_bigdata2018,event_driven2025}. Flowmatic positions its adaptive core unit within this trend: classical preparation supplies auditable tabular baselines; neural checkpoints supply modality-specific predictors selected by explicit routing rules.

\section{Positioning of This Thesis}

The manuscript therefore reports \emph{two linked contributions}: (1)~classical automated preparation with ensemble anomaly detection and tree-based classification; (2)~a production implementation with neural extensions and adaptive routing. Neither layer invalidates the other---Chapter~\ref{chap:results} compares them explicitly.
