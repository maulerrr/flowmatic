\chapter{Conclusion and Future Directions}
\label{chap:conclusion}

\section{Summary of Contributions}

This dissertation presents \textbf{Flowmatic}, an intelligent preparation and inference platform for urban transportation data:
\begin{itemize}
  \item six-dimensional DQI and automated batch cleaning with measured latency;
  \item Dockerised batch and smart-city pipelines with WebSocket event delivery;
  \item eight Hugging Face neural checkpoints under a shared temporal hold-out protocol;
  \item Manual/Auto routing over a documented production registry;
  \item open reproduction scripts (\texttt{run\_phase2\_prep.py}, \texttt{run\_phase3\_core.py}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Key Findings}

Batch preparation on 30\,000 Astana records completes in 406\,ms with quality score 100/100. The Transformer severity model reaches macro-F1 = $0.911 \pm 0.017$; TranAD anomaly reconstruction RMSE is $0.035 \pm 0.004$ on Astana test windows. Compact forecasters (TimesBlock, DLinear) pair with low streaming latency; iTransformer speed forecasting requires retraining or registry retirement.

\section{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Semi-synthetic Astana data; no live agency deployment.
  \item METR-LA and PEMS-BAY ingested but not yet used as external hold-outs.
  \item Router match-rate and probabilistic forecast metrics (CRPS) not reported.
  \item TranAD evaluated on reconstruction RMSE, not event AUROC.
  \item LLM copilot summaries lack formal calibration.
\end{itemize}

\section{Future Work}

Priorities include METR-LA/PEMS hold-out rows, auto-router confusion analysis, AUROC for injected anomalies, extended training for weak slots, and operator studies on Insight Engine outputs. Federated coordination remains a demonstration path.

\section{Concluding Remarks}

Trustworthy ITS analytics requires auditable preparation connected to deployed models. Flowmatic demonstrates that quality assessment, multimodal neural inference, and smart-city orchestration can be delivered as one reproducible platform with published checkpoints and measured operational behaviour.
