\section{Provenance}

The Astana export (\texttt{astana\_synthetic\_data.csv}, 30\,000 rows) is the primary case study for batch preparation (RQ1--RQ2) and Astana-facing neural slots (RQ3). It is \textbf{not} open municipal data.

\textbf{NDA source.} The generator is calibrated on an urban traffic dataset provided under a \textbf{non-disclosure agreement (NDA)} to the research group. Raw records cannot be published or redistributed. The CSV in the Flowmatic repository is a \textbf{semi-synthetic derivative}: it preserves the real schema, field domains, and aggregate behaviour while replacing identifiable values so that thesis and platform experiments remain reproducible without breaching the NDA.

\textbf{Generator specification.} The rules below summarise the data-generation formulae from a companion manuscript (2026, \textbf{unpublished}, in preparation)~\cite{bakytuly2026architectural}, which continues the published Astana microservices study~\cite{bakytuly2026microservices}. The released CSV, schema, and experiment scripts are sufficient to reproduce thesis tables; exact calibration constants for the NDA-derived generator remain part of the companion study and are not required to rerun the reported Flowmatic experiments.

\section{Generation rules (summary)}

Fixed parameters: $N = 30\,000$; rush-hour mixture $\alpha_{\mathrm{rush}} = 0.3$; speed bounds $v_{\min}, v_{\max}$ mapped to \texttt{Speed\_kmh}; vehicle-type probabilities $\mathbf{p}_{\mathrm{type}}$ with $\sum p = 1$.

\textbf{Time.} Fractional day position
\begin{equation}
  t_{\mathrm{frac}} = \frac{h \cdot 3600 + m \cdot 60 + s}{86400}
\end{equation}
is drawn from a peak-biased mixture (weight $\alpha_{\mathrm{rush}}$ on $\mathcal{N}(\mu_{\mathrm{peak}}, \sigma_{\mathrm{peak}})$, otherwise $\mathcal{U}(0,1)$), producing rush-hour elevation in hourly counts.

\textbf{Space.} $(\mathrm{lat}_i, \mathrm{lon}_i)$ are sampled inside the Astana bounding box (road-network sampling in the full specification; the export uses uniform draws inside the bbox subject to Flowmatic validity checks).

\textbf{Density and speed.} $v_i \sim \mathcal{U}(v_{\min}, v_{\max})$; traffic density
\begin{equation}
  \rho_i = \rho_0 + A \sin(2\pi t_{\mathrm{frac},i}) + \Delta_{\mathrm{type}(i)}
\end{equation}
with baseline $\rho_0$, diurnal amplitude $A$, and event-type offset $\Delta_{\mathrm{type}(i)}$. Event types are categorical draws with higher congestion and accident rates in peak bands.

\textbf{Severity.} $s_i$ is assigned by rule from $(\rho_i, v_i, \text{event type})$, not from confirmed incident reports (approx.\ 91\% Low, 7\% High, 2\% Medium in the export). Classifiers measure recoverable structure in the synthetic label, not field-verified injury severity.

\textbf{Preparation stress only.} Table~\ref{tab:prep-stress} injects defects offline on a 10\,000-row subsample (missing values, invalid coordinates, duplicates, stale timestamps at 0--20\%); the pristine 30\,000-row export is unchanged.
